What it does

Four things, done well.

01

Tap to run the real thing

Every tile launches an app, shortcut or command on your computer and shows live status.

  • 16 tile types, plus widgets
  • Sliders and dials for volume, brightness and more
  • Profiles that follow your front app
02

Chain steps into routines

Group repeated actions into a workflow. Watch each step and queue the next one.

  • Live progress, logs and a reply box
  • Schedule a routine for later
  • History of every run and who started it
03

Approve AI before it runs

Assistants propose a fix and the command. You approve or deny from the tablet.

  • Approve once, or reject
  • Works with local CLI assistants on Mac
  • Credentials stay on your computer
04

Control up to four computers

Pair by QR code over Wi-Fi, VPN or USB. Mix their controls in one profile.

  • See which computer owns each tile
  • Revoke any device at any time
  • File sharing and clipboard are opt-in

    Private by design

    You choose what connects.

    • Pair you approveReview and revoke devices.
    • Opt-in sharingFiles and clipboard stay off until you enable them.
    • Local credentialsIntegration keys stay on your computer.
    Does it need an account?

    No NitroDeck account. Connected services and AI tools use their own accounts.

    Which tablets and computers work?

    iPad and Android tablets, with Mac and Windows companions. Some features, like managed AI assistants and USB, are Mac only.

    Can I share a pack?

    Yes. Build one in the generator and open a pull request to add it to the marketplace.
