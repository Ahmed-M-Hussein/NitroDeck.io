How it works

One tap. Four steps. Zero friction.

Your tablet is the remote. A small companion app on your computer does the work, privately on your own network.

  1. 1

    Tap

    Hit a tile on your tablet to open an app, press keys, run a command or start a workflow.

  2. 2

    Secure hop

    Your tablet talks to your computer over Wi-Fi, VPN or USB (Mac). The link is locked to the computer you paired.

  3. 3

    Verified

    The companion app checks the device is trusted, the profile is allowed and the action is in its own layout.

  4. 4

    Done

    It runs locally and the result lights up the tile: running, passed or failed.

What you get

Four superpowers.

The core of NitroDeck, and what each one does for your day.

01

Tap. It happens.

Every tile launches an app, fires a shortcut or runs a command on your computer, then shows the result right on the tile. Group tiles into profiles for each kind of work and let the deck follow the app you're in.

  • 16 tile types, plus live widgets
  • Sliders and dials for volume, brightness and more
  • Profiles that switch with your front app
02

Turn routines into one tap

Chain steps into a workflow, such as typecheck, test, build, with typed variables where you need them. Start it from a tile, watch each step live, queue the next run or schedule it for later.

  • Live progress, logs and a reply box
  • Schedule once or on repeat
  • Full run history with owner and timings
03

AI proposes. You approve.

Choose from 19 assistants and providers, from local tools like Codex and Claude Code to hosted APIs. See the plan and the exact command first, and nothing runs until you say so. Keys and logins stay on your computer.

  • Approve once, or reject
  • Local CLIs, hosted APIs and local runtimes
  • Credentials stay on your computer
04

Four computers. One tablet.

Pair each computer once with a QR code. Then one profile can mix tiles from your Mac, your Windows PC and more, and every tile shows which computer owns it and whether it's online.

  • See which computer owns each tile
  • Revoke any device, any time
  • File and clipboard sharing, opt-in

    Private by design

    You decide what connects.

    • You approve pairingReview and revoke devices any time.
    • Sharing is opt-inFiles and clipboard stay off until you turn them on.
    • Keys stay homeIntegration credentials stay on your computer.
    Do I need an account?

    No NitroDeck account. Connected services and AI tools use their own accounts.

    What works with what?

    iPad and Android tablets, with Mac and Windows companions. USB links and calendar meetings are Mac only for now; USB on Windows isn't built yet.

    Can I share a pack?

    Yes. Build one in the generator and open a pull request to add it to the marketplace.
