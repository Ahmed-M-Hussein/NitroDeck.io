Your devices, working together

A tablet on your desk. Your computers in reach.

Use the mobile app as your control surface and the companion app on each computer you want to control. Connect up to four computers and combine their controls in one profile.

Features vary by desktop platform. The Mac companion supports managed AI assistants and USB connections; Windows supports desktop controls, workflows, media, OBS, and file sharing.

Make it yours

Your tools. Your layout. Your rhythm.

Choose tile names, icons, colours, and sizes. Organize related controls into groups, create profiles for different tasks, and choose a light or dark appearance that suits your desk.

Start with familiar tools

Use shortcut packs for creative apps, browsers, meetings, and media. Add the controls you already reach for.

Build useful routines

Bring repeated actions together in workflows. Follow progress, queue the next task, and schedule routines for later.

Stay in control

See which computer a control belongs to and when it is unavailable. Keep separate profiles for focus, presentations, and creative work.

Confidence at your desk

You choose what connects and what is shared.

Pair devices you trust

Approve the connection between your tablet and computer. Review paired devices and revoke access when you need to.

Share on your terms

File transfer and shared clipboard are opt-in. Enable them on the computers you want to use together.

Keep credentials on your computer

Integration credentials stay with the desktop companion. Connected services and AI providers use their own accounts and terms.

Get started

Make room for a better workflow.

Choose your devices, connect them, and build your first deck.

01 · Choose

Get the apps for your devices

You need the NitroDeck mobile app for your tablet and the companion app for your Mac or Windows computer. Check the release page for available builds.

02 · Connect

Pair your tablet and computer

Open the companion app and use its pairing details to connect your tablet. Approve access on your computer.

03 · Personalize

Add your everyday controls

Start with a few favourite apps and shortcuts. Add profiles, workflows, and other computers as your deck grows.
