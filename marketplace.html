Make a pack for your favourite app

Packs are plain JSON files in the open NitroDeck.io repository. Add a pack and a small metadata file, open a pull request, and automatic checks tell you if anything is off. Packs can only contain safe, typed controls: shortcuts, panels and app launchers. Scripts and commands are never allowed.
